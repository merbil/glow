Interactive: try the popover on the right
  • 👆Click a tile to turn a lamp on or off.
  • ↔︎Drag sideways on a tile to set the brightness. The colored fill follows your finger.
  • 🎨Each tile glows in the lamp's real color. Lamps that are off are quiet grey boxes.
  • ⏱All off ▾ turns everything off now or after 5, 15, 30 or 60 minutes. The countdown runs on the Hue bridge, so it still works if your Mac goes to sleep.
  • 🖱Right-click a tile to rename or hide it in Glow only. Your Hue setup isn't touched.
  • 🌫An unreachable lamp (e.g. switched off at the wall) appears faded.
